Where every ringgit comes from — and goes

Every number here comes straight from Malaysia's audited federal accounts (the Penyata Kewangan Kerajaan Persekutuan), checked against the government's own printed totals down to the sen. In short: revenue has grown, but debt has tripled to 65% of GDP, and a rising share of every ringgit is locked into salaries, pensions and interest before a single service is delivered. Source & method. Federal Government Financial Statements (Penyata Kewangan Kerajaan Persekutuan), audited by the National Audit Department, FY2009–FY2024 — each year taken from its own audited volume, amounts exact Ringgit as printed, reconciled to the government's own printed totals. Operating = Belanja Mengurus; Development = Belanja Pembangunan; a ministry line is a vote (maksud). Budget-vs-actual compares each vote's original estimate (anggaran asal) to actual (sebenar). GDP & population are official DOSM series (OpenDOSM: annual nominal GDP, population, households — ingested as sources/dosm, external to the audited warehouse) — used only for ratios and per-household context. The pension projection and reform simulator are illustrative. RM b = billion; RM tn = trillion. Peer comparisons are IMF/national figures, external and indicative — cross-country debt definitions differ.
