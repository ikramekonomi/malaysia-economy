# PKKP Warehouse — Validation Report (FY2009–FY2024, full-detail extraction — COMPLETE)

**All 16 audited volumes FY2009–FY2024 are now extracted, cross-verified, and consolidated** (~580,000 numeric data points; `fact_opex_detail` alone holds 76,320 object-level rows across the years). Each volume was extracted by a parameterized pipeline, reconciled to its own printed totals, checked by an independent adversarial verifier agent blind to the extraction code, and cross-audited against the adjacent volume's restated comparatives. Every figure carries provenance (source PDF, page); discrepancies are reported, never silently corrected. The one partial is FY2015 (image-only scan — leaf grain OCR-provisional; see its section). The sections below document each volume; the first (FY2024) describes the base methodology in most depth.

Source of the base methodology write-up: 2024_Penyata_Kewangan_Kerajaan_Persekutuan.pdf (447 pages, text layer intact; one glyph-corrupted line in the whole volume, see below). Method: programmatic reconciliation plus independent adversarial verifier agents, per the project rule that discrepancies are reported, never silently corrected. Scope: 27 fact tables, ~8,100 rows, every numeric table in the volume.

## Layer 1 — Programmatic reconciliation (383 checks, all PASS)

Three check suites, persisted in `_validation_checks.csv` (150 core + 73 notes-detail) and `_validation_checks_memoranda.csv` (160 register checks):

- **Statements & notes:** revenue line items reproduce printed category totals and total HASIL in all four value columns; the 53 opex Maksud sum to printed Tanggungan/Bekalan/grand totals; headline figures and the fiscal and financing identities hold for 2024 and 2023; cash, trust, debt, memorandum and commitment tables cross-foot internally and against each other.
- **Detail layers:** all 2,206 Lampiran-1 leaf rows sum to their aktiviti totals (1,068), program totals (237), maksud totals (53, to the sen), jenis totals and the grand total, in every value column; all 923 Lampiran-2 project rows sum to program subtotals, ministry totals and the printed grand total (RM84,011,595,463.78) in all seven numeric columns, and satisfy per-row identities (langsung+pinjaman=jumlah; prior cumulative+2024 actual=2024 cumulative).
- **Registers:** instrument-level debt issues sum to printed instrument totals (TPK 570.8b, MGS 633.8b, etc.) and to Hutang Persekutuan; investments to JUMLAH PELABURAN; guarantees, leases, PFI, compensation and statutory guarantees to their printed totals, both years.
- **15-year panel:** subtotals cross-foot across all 15 years for balance-sheet, revenue, opex-by-object, borrowing-flow, debt-stock, receivable and guarantee blocks; 2024 column ties to the exact per-year fact tables at RM-juta rounding.

Verifier agents, blind to the extraction code, re-derived the data from the PDF (character-coordinate parsing) and compared against the CSVs:

- **Pass 1 (core tables):** every row of the eight statement tables verified; zero numeric discrepancies; five omissions found and fixed, then re-verified CONFIRMED-CLEAN.
- **Pass 2 (agent-extracted notes + registers):** the two extraction agents self-validated (73 + 160 checks); the final verifier spot-verified debt issues (incl. FX rows), investments (incl. USD entities, 1MDB share classes), trust fund statements and sub-funds, guarantees and leases — all match.
- **Pass 3 (detail layers, exhaustive):** re-parsed **every** Lampiran-1 page positionally (all 7 columns, all rows, including the ten 2-up spread pages), **all 1,012** devex project rows across both pages of every spread, and **all 2,220** timeseries values. Findings: one sign error (timeseries Sukuk 2024, caused by a double-printed overlapping glyph cell in the PDF) and one omitted row (the glyph-corrupted line below). Both fixed; additivity re-verified. Salvaged blank-cell rows (9), watermark-overprinted cells (~8) and print-side token concatenations were each individually confirmed correctly aligned.
- **Pass 4 (delta):** fixes independently re-verified.
