# PKKP Data Warehouse — Data Dictionary (FY2009–FY2024, full-detail extraction — COMPLETE)

Sources: *Penyata Kewangan Kerajaan Persekutuan* 2024 (447pp) … 2016 (293pp), 2015 (306pp, no text layer — OCR), 2014 (307pp), 2013 (288pp), 2012 (265pp), 2011 (330pp), 2010 (305pp), 2009 (274pp, bilingual, archaic cash-basis presentation), audited, JANM, BM editions. All 16 volumes are extracted (~580k numeric data points). **FY2015 caveat:** the 2015 volume is an image-only scan; its aggregate/statement/register/panel tables are fully verified (cross-checked against the 2016 volume's 2015 comparatives), but its `fact_opex_detail` object-leaf and `fact_devex_project` project-leaf amounts are OCR-provisional and not reconciled to printed subtotals — use `fact_opex_maksud`/`fact_devex_maksud` (verified) for 2015 ministry-level analysis and see `2015/_unresolved_ocr_cells_2015.csv`. Every numeric table in each volume is extracted. **Multi-year layout:** the root folder holds the 2024 volume's tables; `2023/` … `2009/` hold their volumes' (identical schemas, year-specific columns documented below). `pkkp.duckdb` unions them — each volume contributes its own year plus prior-year comparatives, so a given year can appear from two volumes, distinguished by `source_file`; differences between them are RESTATEMENTS (catalogued per year: `2023/_restatements_2023_vs_2024volume.csv` 82, `2022/…` 152, `2021/…` 219, `2020/…` 218, `2019/…`, `2018/…` 150 findings). Filter `source_file` (or take each year from its own volume) for clean time series. **Older-volume schema notes:** 2016–2020 volumes print 4 opex value columns (Asal/Dipinda/Amaun/%) — `anggaran_tambahan_rm`/`viremen_rm`/`varian_rm` empty, Dipinda→`anggaran_jumlah_rm`; 2016–2019 devex cumulative columns are RMKe-11-era (`terkumpul_rmke11_*`; in the 2016 volume — plan year one — cumulative equals the 2016 actual on every row); the 2018 volume prints 8 Lampiran and the 2016–2017 volumes only 6 (no guarantees-commitment/lease/PFI/grants/compensation registers in 2016–2017; absent in source, not omitted); pre-2019 Nota 6 policy: no premium/discount row, non-nil closing balances (2018: RM15.22b; 2017: RM33.48b; 2016: RM20.32b). The 2017 volume's `fact_loans_receivable` carries an era-specific `hasil_disatukan_rm` column. The 2011–2015 volumes are RMKe-10 era (`terkumpul_rmke10_*`; 2011 is RMKe-10 year 1, cumulative = 2011 actual); the 2009 volume is RMKe-9 (statement E1ai) with a `pindah_ref` transfer-reference column. Panels run `fact_timeseries_2010_2019` … `fact_timeseries_2000_2009` — coverage reaches back to 2000. The 2009–2012 volumes have no per-issue debt register (`fact_debt_issues` absent — debt appears only as by-instrument summary in the notes). **The 2009 volume is structurally distinct** (archaic cash-basis IPSAS: statements named A–G/C1/C2/E1ai not "Lampiran"; opex grain is program+object only, no aktiviti tier; Notes are narrative-only; no performance statement or commitment schedule — these are genuine source absences, not gaps). Use `fact_opex_maksud`/`fact_devex_maksud` for cross-year ministry-level series, which are homogeneous across all 16 volumes. The 2015 volume introduces the GST revenue line (Import + Tempatan) in `fact_revenue`; the 2014 volume uses archaic memorandum labels "Hutang Awam"/"Pinjaman Boleh Dituntut" (later "Hutang Persekutuan"/"Pinjaman dan Pendahuluan Boleh Dituntut").

## Conventions (all tables)

CSVs are the canonical layer; `pkkp.duckdb` is regenerated from them (`python3 pipeline/build_duckdb.py`). Malay labels are canonical (`*_bm`); English glosses only in `dim_maksud.name_en`. Amounts in `*_rm` columns are Ringgit exactly as printed (sen kept where printed); the timeseries panel is `amount_rm_juta` (RM millions, as printed). En-dash (–) in source = nil = 0.0; parenthesised = negative. `has_footnote=True` marks values printed with an asterisk. Every row carries `source_file`, `pdf_page`, `printed_page`, `statement`. Tables that mix detail and subtotal rows carry `row_type` — **SUM only over detail/leaf rows** (`row_type='detail'` or `'object_aktiviti'` or `'project'`) to avoid double counting; subtotal rows are retained deliberately so every printed figure exists in the warehouse.

## Core statements (printed p.59–63)

**fact_financial_position** — Penyata Kedudukan Kewangan: Wang Awam (cash+investments) vs the three consolidated accounts. **fact_cash_statement** — cash receipts/payments; its Hasil block classifies some items differently from Nota 5(a) by design of the source. **fact_financial_performance** — headline aggregates incl. the three financing component lines. **fact_memorandum** — memorandum assets/liabilities summary.

## Notes (printed p.67–81)

**fact_loan_account_statement** — Nota 6's Penyata Akaun Pinjaman Disatukan roll-forward (borrowing receipts/repayments by DN/LN and multilateral/bilateral assistance, premium/discount adjustment, transfers to Kumpulan Wang Pembangunan/COVID-19, closing balance). **fact_revenue_account_statement** — Nota 5's Penyata Akaun Hasil Disatukan roll-forward (opening balance, revenue by category, opex by object class *in the accrual-note presentation* — its Pemberian figure differs from both the cash statement and Lampiran 1 by the transfer/footnote effects, all three presentations are retained — the surplus transfer, closing balance). **fact_revenue** — Nota 5(a) revenue by classification, 3-level hierarchy (`level` 0–3), original/revised budget, actual + printed `pct_anggaran`, prior year. **fact_opex_maksud** — Nota 5(b), the 53 votes (T.01–B.64). **fact_cash_components** (Nota 3), **fact_investment_summary** (Nota 4), **fact_trust_fund_detail** (Nota 7: Kumpulan Wang Pembangunan & COVID-19 statements, 50 Pelbagai KWAK sub-funds, Amanah Am/Khas, deposits), **fact_memo_assets_detail** (Nota 9: receivables & memo investments), **fact_debt** (Nota 10: instruments, PFI, commitments incl. subtotals — filter `kind`), **fact_commitment_detail** (Nota 10 splits: jaminan DN/LN, pajakan, bayaran balik cukai incl. the GST row, pelbagai liabiliti items), **fact_trust_accounts** (Nota 7 top table), **fact_other_notes** (Nota 11–13 inline figures).

## Lampiran detail

**fact_opex_detail** (Lampiran 1, 4,485 rows) — operating expenditure at full grain: `jenis` (Tanggungan/Bekalan) → `maksud_code` → `program_code/name` → `aktiviti_code/name` → `objek_code/class` (10000 Emolumen … 50000 Lain). Seven value columns: `anggaran_asal`, `anggaran_tambahan` (supplementary), `viremen` (virement), `anggaran_jumlah`, `sebenar`, `prestasi_pct`, `varian`. Leaf grain = `row_type='object_aktiviti'` (2,206 rows); recap/subtotal row_types: object_program, jumlah_aktiviti, jumlah_program, jumlah_objek_maksud, jumlah_maksud, jumlah_objek_jenis, jumlah_jenis, jumlah_objek_keseluruhan, jumlah_besar.

**fact_devex_project** (Lampiran 2, 1,012 rows) — development expenditure at project grain: `projek_code` (5-digit) within `maksud_code`. `program_bm` links a project to its printed program grouping ONLY where the linkage is arithmetically verified (tagged projects sum exactly to the program's Jumlah row — 51 groups, 321 projects); it is empty where the printed grouping is ambiguous (two-level nestings), in which case row order still preserves the printed sequence. Value columns: `kos_keseluruhan` (approved cost), `terkumpul_rmke12_prior`, anggaran and sebenar each split langsung/pinjaman/jumlah, and `terkumpul_rmke12`. row_type: project (923) / program_subtotal / ministry_total / grand_total. **fact_devex_maksud** — ministry-level rollup (kept for convenience; identical totals).

**fact_loans_receivable** (Lampiran 3) — loans/advances by borrower (states, statutory bodies, companies), with the 2024 balance split by funding source (pembangunan/amanah). Note: source numbering skips category "IV". **fact_investments** (Lampiran 4) — 137 share-class lines across government shareholdings, with share capital and nominal values in original currency (`_ccy`/`_amt`), holding %, book value. **fact_debt_issues** (Lampiran 5) — all 96 individual debt series (Bil Perbendaharaan, 33 TPK, 38 MGS, market borrowings, bilateral/multilateral lenders) with rates, maturities, FX where printed. **fact_pfi_liabilities** (Lampiran 6) — PFI tranches with rates and maturities. **fact_guarantees** (Lampiran 7) — the 14 guaranteed entities, DN/LN split. **fact_guarantee_repayment_grants** — grant channelling for guaranteed-loan repayments by agency. **fact_finance_leases** (Lampiran 8) — 86 lease lines by sector/category. **fact_compensation_commitments** (Lampiran 9). **fact_statutory_guarantees** (Lampiran 10) — incl. deposit-insurance-type statutory guarantees (2024 total RM1.75 trillion; do not sum with Lampiran 7 — overlapping coverage by design).

## Year-specific tables

**fact_covid_fund_detail** (2020–2023 volumes, their Lampiran 3) — the COVID-19 fund's program-level expenditure register across its whole life: 2020 volume = first year (Akta 830; tambahan/tarik columns, no prior cumulative; closing balance RM761,369), 2021 volume = live two-page spread with an extra `tambahan_rm` column (`terkumpul_hingga_2020_rm`/`terkumpul_hingga_2021_rm`), 2022 volume = live (`terkumpul_hingga_2021_rm`/`terkumpul_hingga_2022_rm`), 2023 volume = wind-down. The 2022 volume reports the fund live (columns `terkumpul_hingga_2021_rm`/`terkumpul_hingga_2022_rm`; 2022 spend RM30.98b, cumulative RM106.7b); the 2023 volume reports the wind-down (`terkumpul_hingga_2022_rm`/`terkumpul_hingga_2023_rm`; fund closed 31.12.2022). The union carries both — the shared `terkumpul_hingga_2022_rm` column is cumulative-through-2022 from either volume; year-specific cumulative columns are NULL for the other volume's rows. Both volumes renumber later Lampiran +1 accordingly. **fact_timeseries_2008_2022** and **fact_timeseries_2009_2023** (each volume's own panel) complement **fact_timeseries_2010_2024**; overlapping cells agree except catalogued RM1-juta restatements and one RM397-juta 2014 cell (see the restatement files).

## 15-year panel

**fact_timeseries_2010_2024** (printed p.266–271) — 148 line items × 15 years (2010–2024) in RM juta: balance-sheet series, consolidated-account roll-forwards, revenue/opex by class, borrowing flows by instrument, debt stocks, guarantees. Rounded to RM juta by the source; use the per-year fact tables for exact figures. Each volume ships its own panel: `fact_timeseries_2009_2023`, `fact_timeseries_2008_2022` (15-year), and `fact_timeseries_2012_2021`, `fact_timeseries_2011_2020` (the 2021/2020 volumes print 10-year panels). Together coverage reaches 2008; the panels are the cross-check backbone as older volumes are extracted. Note: the 2020 volume's panel prints a sign-convention flip in its financing block for the 2018–2020 columns — faithful to print.

**Year-specific devex columns:** the 2021 volume marks the RMKe-11→RMKe-12 transition (its `terkumpul_rmke12_rm` equals `sebenar_jumlah_rm` for every project, identity-validated; the left cumulative column is RMKe-11-era). The 2020 volume's cumulative columns are named `terkumpul_rmke11_*`. The 2020 volume prints only 4 opex value columns, so `anggaran_tambahan_rm`/`viremen_rm`/`varian_rm` are empty there and the revised budget (Dipinda) maps to `anggaran_jumlah_rm`; its Lampiran 1 was OCR-extracted (no text layer) and verifier-audited — see VALIDATION_REPORT.

## Dimensions & meta

**dim_maksud** — (maksud_code, year) with jenis, BM name, EN gloss, `note` (editorial annotations, e.g. P.32 abolished ministry). **meta_validation_checks / meta_validation_checks_memoranda** — all programmatic reconciliation checks with extracted vs printed values.

## Known source anomalies (preserved, never corrected)

RM1 inconsistency in Hasil WP 2023 between cash statement and Nota 5(a); "TRANFORMASI" typo in Lampiran 2 (join on codes, not names); restated 2023 comparatives flagged in notes (finance leases, pajakan); Nota 10 rounds Lampiran figures by up to RM0.30; Lampiran 3 skips category number IV.

## Remaining exclusions

Narrative-only content (Laporan Akauntan Negara commentary and its charts — figures restate the statements), Sijil Ketua Audit Negara, glossaries, organisation charts, and the English half of the volume (duplicate). Every numeric data table in the BM statements/notes/lampiran/appendix is extracted.
